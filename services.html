Boilers & Hydronic Heating

Few things beat the steady, draft-free warmth of a well-designed hydronic system. We install, replace and service high-efficiency boilers, radiant floor heating and hot-water (hydronics) systems for homes throughout Hamilton and the surrounding region.

If your boiler is leaking, short-cycling, or simply old and expensive to run, replacing it with a modern condensing unit can dramatically cut your heating bills while keeping every room evenly comfortable. We'll size the system properly, set up your zones, and make sure the install is clean and built to last.

  • High-efficiency boiler installs & replacements
  • Radiant & hydronic heating expertise
  • Even, quiet, draft-free comfort
  • Lower energy bills & fewer breakdowns
Replacing an old boiler?

Most boilers last 15–20 years. If yours is near the end, we'll give you a straight comparison of repair vs. replacement — no pressure, just the smart move for your home and budget.

Heat Pumps

A heat pump is one efficient system that both heats your home in winter and cools it in summer — making it one of the smartest upgrades available to Ontario homeowners today.

Modern cold-climate heat pumps perform beautifully through Hamilton winters and can slash your energy use compared with older systems. We install, service and repair every type — air-to-water heat pumps for hydronic and in-floor heating, ducted central-air heat pumps for whole-home comfort, and ductless mini-splits for room-by-room control. Replacing an aging furnace-and-AC pair with a single heat pump (or a hybrid setup) often qualifies for valuable rebates, and we'll handle everything end to end.

  • Heating & cooling in one system
  • Cold-climate models for Ontario winters
  • Lower carbon footprint & energy costs
  • Rebate guidance included
Ready to upgrade?

If your furnace and AC are both aging, replacing them with a heat pump can be the most cost-effective move long term. We'll lay out the options honestly so you can decide with confidence.
